## Segments & tracks

| Segment | Track | Today-buyer? | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Operating mines & processing plants (esp. ramp-up / recently commissioned) | **Beachhead A — co-primary** | Yes | Live plant data exists; 8-week diagnostic runs immediately; ramp-up pain is sharpest. |

**Decision (Q1):** Operating/ramp-up plants AND greenfield start-ups are both primary beachheads. Consequence accepted: two distinct sales motions and two wedge offers.

## Wedge offers by motion

- **Operating / ramp-up plants:** the standard **eight-week diagnostic** on the client's own data → measured baseline → annual subscription + integration.
- **Greenfield start-ups (two-stage):** Stage 1 — paid, fixed-scope **commissioning-readiness / data-architecture review** during construction (design ONE's data model in before first ore). Stage 2 — at first ore, auto-convert to the eight-week diagnostic. Hook: designing the decision layer in at commissioning beats retrofitting it after eating ramp-up losses.

## Qualifying signals (nightly finder rules)

Score accounts; a signal must predict BOTH pain and ability to buy. Commodity-neutral, ranked on triggers.

**Operating / ramp-up plants (highest intent):**
- Recently commissioned or ramping a new plant/expansion in last ~18 months.
- Publicly reported throughput/recovery shortfalls, guidance cuts, unplanned downtime/outage.
- Mentions of historians / SCADA / digital twin / "digital transformation" (proves connectable data exists).
- Single-site or few-site producer (faster decision; majors feed the multiplier track).

**Greenfield (design-in):**
- Financing closed / FID reached / construction or commissioning announced for next 12–24 months.

**Multiplier / channel (lower priority):**
- Funds/owners announcing portfolio-wide operational-excellence or digital mandates; OEM/EPC new plant-automation contracts.

## Pain-point taxonomy (forum search terms + messaging angles)

Operators' real words, not brochure language. Finder tags each lead with the pain # it matches.

1. **Data trapped in OEM/vendor silos** — "can't get data out of [OEM] without their portal," "no API," "locked in the PLC/SCADA."
2. **Decisions lag the process** — "running off spreadsheets," "shift report too late," "we find out next morning."
3. **Ramp-up not hitting nameplate** — "can't reach design throughput," "stuck at X% of nameplate," "commissioning dragging."
4. **Recovery / grade drift** — "recovery dropping and don't know why," "grade swinging," "losing value to tails."
5. **Unplanned downtime / reactive maintenance** — "another unexpected failure," "no early warning," "PM keeps slipping."
6. **Too many disconnected dashboards** — "five systems, no single view," "got PI/Grafana but still blind," "swivel-chair between tools."

## Disqualifiers (finder auto-excludes)

- Exploration-stage / juniors with no process plant (greenfield counts only once financed/FID'd).
- Care-and-maintenance, closures, assets being wound down.
- Pure commodity-price / market / stock news with no operational angle.
- Service/supplier press dressed as customer news (OEM announcing a sale → route to channel track, not prospecting).
- Distressed/bankrupt operators with no capacity for a paid engagement.

**Coal: INCLUDED** (per Steven) — treated like any other commodity; not excluded on ESG grounds. Commodity-neutral stands.

## Targeting / buying committee — BOTH top-down and bottom-up (run in parallel)

Land-and-expand from the plant AND secure an exec sponsor at the same time; converge in the middle.

**Bottom-up (champion / entry):** Plant Manager, Processing/Metallurgy Manager, GM Operations — own throughput/recovery, feel the pain in their numbers. Lead with the matched pain #.
**Top-down (exec sponsor / economic buyer):** COO or VP Operations (single/few-site); Operating Partner or portfolio Asset Manager (funds/owners). Frame ROI + portfolio leverage.
**Technical validator (arm, don't pitch):** OT/Controls Engineer, Process Control lead — bless "read-only over segmented OT/IT." Give them the security one-pager early.
**Greenfield variant:** Project Director / Commissioning Manager / Owner's-team lead during the build.
**Security gatekeeper (pre-empt, never pitch):** CISO / IT Security — concern is the read-only OT link.

Outreach: simultaneous threads to the Plant/Processing Manager (bottom-up) and COO/VP Ops or Operating Partner (top-down), each referencing the other, with the OT-security posture surfaced for the validator.
