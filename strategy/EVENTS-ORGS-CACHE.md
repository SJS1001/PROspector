## Blind spots (events strategy + how to make it work)

1. **Booths rarely pay back for an early small team.** A MINExpo/IMARC stand is five figures + travel. *Make it work:* go for **speaking slots, GMG working-group presence, and pre-booked side meetings**, not exhibiting. Your credibility lever is a talk titled like your contrarian posts ("You don't have a sensor problem"), not a pop-up banner.
2. **Timing — you've missed most 2026 T1 events** (it's June; MetPlant, CMP, Euro Mine Expo, GMG Helsinki passed). *Make it work:* H2 2026 = MINExpo (channel), Mining & Critical Minerals Europe, IMARC. Lock the **2027 Nordic/processing** calendar now (CMP Jan, Euro Mine Expo, GMG Helsinki, Nordic Forum) while you have lead time and reference proximity.
3. **No lead-capture system = events evaporate.** Badge scans and business cards die in a drawer. *Make it work:* stand up the lightweight **CRM** first; every event contact → CRM → certified → enrich. Events become a *source* for the same pipeline, not a separate effort.
4. **You're underusing standards/credibility bodies — your single biggest cheap win.** Interoperability is your pitch; **GMG** is the room where operators, OEMs and the digital-twin consortium debate exactly that. *Make it work:* join GMG, get into the Interoperability / Data Access working group, contribute. That's warm relationships + authority for the price of membership, year-round, no booth.
5. **"Conference tourism" risk.** Flying to events without a per-event goal burns cash. *Make it work:* every event needs a written objective before you book — target accounts attending, # meetings to pre-arrange, one talk/panel pitched, follow-up plan.
6. **Events should feed the machine.** Exhibitor lists, speaker lists, and sponsor lists are pre-qualified account lists. *Make it work:* point the nightly agent at each target event's exhibitor/speaker page; it tags them by track and dedups against this cache so we never re-pull the same list.
7. **Nordic reference proximity is a wasting asset.** Your Engebø/Nordic Mining story lands hardest at Nordic events (Euro Mine Expo, GMG Helsinki, Nordic Forum). *Make it work:* anchor 2027 planning around the Nordic circuit while the reference is fresh and local.

## Recommended first moves (cheap, high-leverage)
1. **Join GMG** and get into the Interoperability working group. *(year-round; do now)*
2. **Pick ONE H2-2026 event** to attend with a goal — likely **IMARC (Sydney, Oct)** for ops buyers or **Mining & Critical Minerals Europe (London, Oct)** for proximity.
3. **Pitch a talk** for a 2027 Nordic/processing event (Euro Mine Expo / GMG Helsinki / CMP) now — CFPs open months ahead.
4. **Stand up the CRM** so every event/forum contact lands in the same certified→enrich pipeline.
