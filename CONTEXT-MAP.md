# Context Map

## Contexts

- [PROspector Platform](./CONTEXT.md) — reusable company, product, market-play, prospect, governance, and outreach language
- [ONE for Mining Market Play](./plays/one-for-mining/CONTEXT.md) — mining-specific migration seed for Digitalrain's private Company Workspace
- [ONE for Mining Claim Language](./strategy/CONTEXT.md) — customer-facing migration seed and Claim Guardrails for Digitalrain

## Relationships

- **PROspector Platform → ONE for Mining Market Play**: the platform supplies the reusable workflow; the Market Play supplies mining-specific Customer Profiles, Targets, Motions, Signals, and qualification rules.
- **ONE for Mining Claim Language → ONE for Mining Market Play**: the claim-language context constrains generated mining outreach and sales assets.
- **Market Plays remain isolated**: Product facts may be shared deliberately, but customer evidence, qualification, and outreach do not cross Market Plays unless explicitly promoted to shared Product knowledge.
- **Migration boundary**: Digitalrain's confirmed knowledge is imported into its private Company Workspace; live Prospects, contacts, outreach, Suppression records, credentials, and private documents do not belong in Git.
