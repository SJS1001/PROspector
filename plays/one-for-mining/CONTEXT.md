# ONE for Mining Market Play

The canonical market-specific language for selling Digitalrain's ONE Product into mining. Reusable workflow language remains in the PROspector Platform context; customer-facing proof language remains in the ONE for Mining Claim Language context.

## Language

### Targets

**Site**:
A specific operating mine or processing plant belonging to an Account. Sites belonging to the same Account remain distinct Targets because their pains, buyers, data, and timing differ.
_Avoid_: account, company

**Project**:
A specific pre-operational mining development, construction, or commissioning initiative belonging to an Account.
_Avoid_: operating site, account

### Motions

**Operating Motion**:
A direct sales Motion centred on an operating or ramping Site with current plant data and a present operational pain.

**Greenfield Motion**:
A direct sales Motion centred on a financed or committed Project approaching construction or commissioning.

**Channel Motion**:
A partnership Motion centred on an OEM, EPC, or integrator that could introduce, embed, or deliver ONE for Mining.

**Portfolio Motion**:
A multiplier Motion centred on an owner, investor, or fund that could extend ONE for Mining across multiple Sites or Projects.
_Avoid_: treating Channel or Portfolio Motions as site-level sales Prospects
