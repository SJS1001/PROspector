# Neutral HVAC review-request SOP

## Eligibility

Send a request only when all are true:

- a genuine covered interaction is complete;
- a neutral rule selects the interaction without using satisfaction or predicted
  sentiment;
- a stable interaction/request key proves no duplicate was sent;
- cross-channel cooldown and frequency limits pass;
- no opt-out or durable suppression applies;
- the contact is lawful for the channel and jurisdiction;
- the customer is not pressured on premises and no staff quota applies.

## Prohibited

- fake, employee, family, agency, competitor, or AI-generated reviews;
- gifts, discounts, payment, entries, or services in exchange for a review,
  sentiment, revision, or removal;
- asking only customers thought to be happy;
- requesting stars, positivity, keywords, location, service, technician name, or
  scripted wording;
- blocking negative feedback or conditioning resolution on review changes;
- posting through the customer's device/account;
- exposing private case details in a response.

## Request template

> Thank you for choosing [Business]. If you would like to share an honest review
> of your experience, you can do so here: [platform link]. Feedback is optional,
> and we welcome it whether your experience was positive or negative. For help
> with an unresolved service issue, contact [service contact].

## Audit record

- stable interaction and request key;
- eligibility basis and neutral rule/version;
- request timestamp, channel, template, and platform;
- prior request count and cooldown/frequency result;
- opt-out/suppression state and reason;
- response and escalation owner without unnecessary customer details.

Recheck the current platform policy and the contractor's communication,
privacy, testimonial, and incentive rules before use.
