## Voice of customer — pain signals appendix

Direct, company-attributable forum threads were thin/hard to index this run (Reddit and 911Metallurgist searches returned technical reference pages rather than dated operator posts). The strongest verbatim-adjacent pain narrative came from mineral-processing trade press, and it maps cleanly onto the ONE pain taxonomy:

1. **Data trapped in silos + decisions lagging (#1, #2, #6).** An industry account of a plant metallurgist's morning describes a flotation circuit monitored on an OEM platform, crusher/mill data locked in a login-gated historian, and lab results on a separate system and cycle — the metallurgist spends the first hour reconciling figures, and by the time the report reaches the plant manager the newest data point is already hours old. *(Mipac / MINE Magazine, Jul 2026)* — [source](https://www.mipac.com.au/insights/operational-data-integration-in-mineral-processing/)

2. **Recovery drift is a chronic, hands-on troubleshooting burden (#4).** 911Metallurgist's flotation-recovery optimization material frames recovery loss as an ongoing "prioritize the problem, pursue process stability, implement incremental changes" cycle — the exact loop ONE is meant to shorten. — [source](https://www.911metallurgist.com/blog/flotation-circuit-recovery-optimization/)

3. **Reliability/downtime dominates loss (#5).** Insurer loss data cited across the sector (>US$15B gross claims) attributes the majority of mining property losses to machinery breakdowns and process failures — reinforcing the reactive-maintenance wedge. — [source](https://www.globalminingreview.com/mining/01052026/minings-biggest-risk-isnt-the-disaster-its-the-stopwatch/)
