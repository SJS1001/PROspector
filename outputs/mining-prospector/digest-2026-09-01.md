## Voice of customer (verbatim, with sources)

1. "tanks and sumps spilling over, pipes/chutes plugging, pumps/control valves operating at 100% most of the time and increases in unplanned downtime" — symptoms of a bottlenecked concentrator. [Burns & McDonnell](https://blog.burnsmcd.com/keep-your-mine-humming-by-optimizing-and-debottlenecking-the-concentrator)
2. "treat the cause, not just the effect" — 911Metallurgist's rule for concentrator troubleshooting from grinding to flotation. [911Metallurgist](https://www.911metallurgist.com/blog/concentrator-troubleshooting-from-grinding-to-flotation/)
3. "historian modules for long-term data storage, diagnostics, and model-based control" — what a flotation SCADA stack is expected to include. [Farmonaut](https://farmonaut.com/mining/mineral-processing-scada-flotation-cip-plant-price-guide)
4. "data-driven control strategies via SCADA routinely achieve energy savings of 8-15% and reagent savings up to 10% compared to manual operations." [Farmonaut](https://farmonaut.com/mining/mineral-processing-scada-flotation-cip-plant-price-guide)
5. "digital twin technologies in mining integrate enterprise systems like historians, computerized machine maintenance systems (CMMSs), and data from field operations" — the exact silos ONE unifies. [ScienceDirect review](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2950555024000582)
6. "effective maintenance management systems can cut unplanned downtime by 30-50%" — the size of the reactive-maintenance prize (pain #5). [AMAC](https://amaccompany.com/mining-downtime-prevention-strategies-2026/)
