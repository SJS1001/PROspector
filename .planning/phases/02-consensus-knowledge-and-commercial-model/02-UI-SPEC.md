# Phase 2 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for the authoritative commercial hierarchy, Consensus Interview, versioned knowledge, drift, and replacement workflow. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher for verification by gsd-ui-checker.

---

## Scope and Sources

Phase 2 replaces the Phase 1 Knowledge fixture with an admitted, owner-scoped knowledge workspace. It must make commercial scope, provenance, authority, concurrency, and downstream impact understandable before the owner acts.

Source-of-truth decisions:

- `02-CONTEXT.md`: exact commercial hierarchy and ownership boundaries; research-first interview; separate immutable answer and confirmation actions; proposed/confirmed knowledge; concurrency conflicts; dependency-scoped drift; immutable replacement activation.
- `REQUIREMENTS.md` and `ROADMAP.md`: one-question interview, Company-wide identity versus Market Play-specific state, explicit promotion, historical snapshots, approval invalidation, and selective high-risk pauses.
- `docs/DIRECTION.md` and `docs/IMPLEMENTATION-SPEC.md`: accepted product vocabulary, seeded hierarchy, dependency chain, reusable-knowledge order, and active-configuration replacement behavior.
- `01-UI-SPEC.md`, `site/app/prospector-app.tsx`, and `site/app/globals.css`: established manual design system, shell, focus treatment, panel language, typography, responsive behavior, and live interview patterns.

Out of scope:

- Product readiness, Market Discovery, Profile readiness, schedules, Runner assignments, prospecting, qualification, enrichment, paid work, outreach, Gmail, calling, CRM handoff, workspace export, and restore.
- Creating Runs, Accounts, Signals, Contacts, Candidates, or Prospects from proposed-knowledge promotion.
- Invitations, workspace switching, autonomous confirmation, silent scope inference, or in-place mutation of confirmed knowledge or an active typed configuration.
- Initializing shadcn, adding a component library, or using third-party registry blocks.

## Information Architecture

### Entry and navigation

1. Keep **Knowledge** as one primary navigation destination; do not add four new items to the dark application rail.
2. Within Knowledge, render a labelled local navigation row in this fixed order: **Commercial Model**, **Interview**, **Knowledge Library**, **Drift & Replacements**.
3. A new or incomplete workspace opens on **Interview** and shows exactly one current decision. An established workspace returns to the last owner-selected Knowledge view, unless an unresolved high-risk drift or stale-confirmation conflict requires attention; in that case show a visible banner with a link, but do not steal focus or auto-navigate.
4. Keep **Pilot Status** available. Existing later-phase destinations stay visibly labelled as synthetic/disabled and no Phase 2 action may enable their operational controls.
5. The controlled-pilot banner remains visible. Use: **Commercial knowledge is live. Discovery, prospecting, contacts, schedules, exports, credentials, paid work, and outbound effects remain disabled.**

### Shared scope context

Every Knowledge view shows the current hierarchy path immediately below the page heading:

`Digitalrain / ONE / ONE for Mining / Operating / {Offer when selected}`

- Each segment is a real button or link with a minimum 44px target; the current segment has `aria-current="page"`.
- A scope change is explicit. Never infer a destination from the last edited record or a reused source.
- When no descendant is selected, show **No Market Play selected** or the corresponding missing level, not a placeholder entity.
- Any proposal, question, knowledge version, drift item, or replacement card repeats its destination scope in visible text. Breadcrumb context alone is insufficient authority evidence.

### View structure

**Commercial Model** uses a desktop two-column layout: a 320px hierarchy tree and a flexible entity-detail panel. On mobile, the tree precedes the detail panel. Render the exact hierarchy `Company -> Product -> Market Play -> Customer Profile -> Offer`; do not flatten Market Play and Customer Profile into tags.

**Interview** retains the current flexible question card plus 300–320px scope sidebar. The main column contains only the active question or its separate confirmation snapshot, never a chat transcript or a list of upcoming questions.

**Knowledge Library** starts with two explicit status tabs, **Proposed** and **Confirmed**, followed by scope and kind filters. Proposed is first when reviewable items exist; otherwise Confirmed is first. Tabs expose text and counts, not color alone.

**Drift & Replacements** lists unresolved drift first, then replacement candidates, then immutable activation history. Resolved drift and inactive historical configurations are collapsed behind a native disclosure, not omitted.

## Commercial Model Contract

### Hierarchy tree

Render nodes in stable parent-child order with type, name, and lifecycle/knowledge summary:

- Company: **Digitalrain**.
- Product: **ONE**.
- Market Plays: **ONE for Mining** and later owner-created siblings such as ONE for Marine. Shared capability, delivery, and roadmap truth remains at Product scope.
- Customer Profiles: **Operating** and **Greenfield** under ONE for Mining; Greenfield remains **Draft / nurture**.
- Offers: concrete entry points nested under the applicable Customer Profile.

The tree must visually distinguish the entity type from its name. Expansion state is local UI state; selecting a node updates the detail view without changing authority. Creation/editing flows open an inline panel, create Draft hierarchy or Proposed Knowledge only, and show the exact destination before submission.

### Entity detail

The detail header contains entity type, name, parent path, lifecycle, current confirmed-knowledge count, proposed count, and unresolved-drift count. Beneath it show the ownership categories exactly:

| Scope | Owned knowledge shown |
|-------|-----------------------|
| Product | Capabilities, limitations, delivery, proof, ownership, claim guardrails |
| Market Play | Market, problem, audience, language, evidence policy, offer context |
| Customer Profile | Fit, disqualifiers, roles, signals, rubric, proof/contact/outreach policy, schedule, timezone, output target |
| Offer | Concrete entry point and the Profile/Market Play context it belongs to |

Show **Company-wide identity** and **Market Play-scoped relationship** as a persistent scope legend:

- Organization and Contact identity: Company-wide.
- Account, Target, relevance, evidence, qualification, and outreach: Market Play/Profile scoped.

In Phase 2 this legend explains the model only. **Add account**, **Add contact**, **Create prospect**, **Run discovery**, and equivalent operational controls are absent; if an existing fixture control remains visible, it uses native `disabled` and adjacent copy **Available in a later governed phase.**

## Consensus Interview Contract

### Active-question composition

Render these blocks in this fixed order:

1. Metadata: **QUESTION {N} · {SCOPE TYPE} · REVISION {revision}**, destination path, and prerequisite knowledge versions.
2. One decision-bearing question as the only `h2` in the card.
3. **Evidence**: one card per factual finding with source title/domain, source type, publication/event date when known, retrieval time, excerpt, and source reference. If research found no reliable evidence, state that directly.
4. **Inference**: its own labelled neutral card with the sentence **This is an inference, not a sourced fact.**
5. **Recommendation**: the existing dark-green recommendation panel, including the recommended value and rationale.
6. **Your answer**: answer controls and an editable correction/rescope field only when selected.
7. A boundary note: **Saving an answer does not create Confirmed Knowledge. A separate owner confirmation is required.**

Never mix evidence and inference in one paragraph. Never label a recommendation as evidence. Source links open in a new tab with an accessible warning and must not include untrusted HTML.

### Two-stage decision flow

Stage 1 records an immutable answer snapshot:

- Answer choices: **Use recommendation**, **Write correction**, **Change scope**.
- Correction requires a replacement value and reason. Rescope requires an explicit destination path and reason.
- Primary CTA: **Submit answer for confirmation**.
- While saving: **Submitting answer…**; disable all answer controls without replacing the content.
- Success advances to Stage 2; it does not show a green Confirmed badge.

Stage 2 shows the exact submitted answer, question revision, proposal digest, prerequisite versions, and destination scope in a read-only review card. Provide exactly these owner actions:

- **Accept** — append the proposed value as a new Knowledge Version.
- **Reject** — record rejection and create no confirmed version.
- **Correct** — reveal required corrected value and reason, then **Record correction**; append a new Knowledge Version.
- **Rescope** — reveal required destination and reason, then **Record rescope**; append a new Knowledge Version at the confirmed destination.

The Stage 2 heading is **Confirm submitted answer** and its note is **This action is separate from answer submission and is recorded against the exact snapshot shown.** Do not use generic **Confirm** or **Save** labels. Once a decision succeeds, replace controls with the authoritative result and immutable references; do not leave enabled buttons that could appear repeatable.

### Active-question and history behavior

- At most one question is labelled **Active** per session. Previous, rejected, superseded, and unconfirmed questions appear only in a read-only **Decision history** disclosure with status, lineage, and timestamps.
- A reload or idempotent retry re-renders the same authoritative Active question or recorded decision. Never increment a visible question number optimistically.
- The existing historian data-readiness decision remains Confirmed when its bound snapshot is valid. Legacy-unbound history remains quarantined and uses the established **Review required** state; it is never silently upgraded.

## Knowledge and Provenance Contract

### Proposed Knowledge

Each Proposed card contains, in order:

1. **Proposed** status badge and knowledge kind.
2. Proposed value or bounded summary.
3. Exact destination scope.
4. Origin: upload, import, research, owner edit, same-Company reuse, same-Product reuse, or allowlisted package.
5. Provenance: source title/reference, custody path, retrieved/imported time, and contributing actor/system.
6. Privacy classification, license/use restriction, and reuse eligibility.
7. Difference from current Confirmed Knowledge, or **No confirmed value at this scope**.
8. Required owner action and immutable proposal reference.

Use the sentence **Proposed Knowledge has no authority until you promote it.** on every proposed-detail view. Upload/import completion says **Proposal created** rather than **Knowledge saved**.

Promotion is a separate owner review. Use **Review proposal** to open the exact snapshot, then the four actions **Accept**, **Reject**, **Correct**, and **Rescope** under the same semantics as interview confirmation. Every destination requires confirmation, including reused knowledge. Cross-Company packages must visibly show the allowlist basis and preserved license; contacts, prospects, outreach, suppression, secrets, and unapproved private sources never appear as promotable content.

### Confirmed Knowledge

Each Confirmed card contains:

- **Confirmed** status badge, current value, scope, kind, immutable Knowledge Version ID, owner decision, confirmation time in America/Toronto, and audit reference.
- Provenance chain and the proposal/answer it came from.
- **Supersedes** and **Superseded by** links when applicable.
- Dependencies, if any, without implying that a dependency grants operational authority.

Confirmed Knowledge is read-only. **Edit** is labelled **Propose change** and creates a new Proposed item beside the current confirmed version. Never place confirmed values in an editable form and never overwrite a version in place.

## Drift and Replacement Contract

### Drift cards

Every drift card contains:

1. Risk label: **Standard drift** or **High-risk drift** with the challenged kind.
2. Current confirmed value versus proposed value.
3. Source and provenance.
4. Explicit dependency path: `source -> knowledge version -> typed configuration -> affected artifact`.
5. Affected counts grouped by artifact type and status.
6. Current containment: **No operational effect**, **Affected outbound paused**, or **Operational effects remain disabled in this pilot**.
7. Owner actions **Accept**, **Reject**, **Correct**, and **Rescope**.

High-risk applies only to capability, proof point, claim guardrail, offer, or suppression. It uses amber emphasis plus the text **High-risk drift**; do not use a red destructive treatment merely because risk is high. Only dependency-reached outbound is labelled paused. Never show a Company-wide pause unless every artifact is actually reached by the recorded graph.

### Impact preview

Accepting drift against an entity with an active typed configuration first opens a dedicated, non-modal impact preview. It must show:

- Current Knowledge Version and proposed replacement version.
- Current active configuration ID/digest and candidate replacement ID/digest.
- Exact changed fields.
- Future schedule roll-forward, in-flight historical-proposal treatment, affected unreviewed requalification, invalidated approvals/packages/messages, and reactivation requirements, each as **Affected**, **Not affected**, or **Not present**.
- Contacted/exported history explicitly labelled **Preserved on original configuration**.
- The recorded dependency paths supporting every affected count.

CTA **Create replacement candidate** appends the new knowledge/configuration candidate but does not activate it. On success, navigate to a replacement card with **Candidate — not active** status.

### Replacement activation

Activation is a second, separate transaction. The replacement card repeats the full impact summary and uses primary CTA **Activate replacement**. Immediately above it show:

**Activation preserves the current snapshot as history, moves future work to this immutable replacement, and invalidates only the dependent approvals shown above. It does not authorize any later operational effect.**

During mutation use **Activating replacement…**. On success show **Replacement active**, activated timestamp, owner/audit reference, new digest, and a link to the preserved prior snapshot. If there is no active configuration, state **No replacement activation is required; this knowledge version is authoritative for future readiness work.**

Phase 2 must keep later operational controls disabled even after replacement activation. Activation may update configuration authority and recorded containment only; it must not start discovery, prospecting, schedules, enrichment, exports, or outbound.

## Concurrency and State Contract

| State | Contract |
|-------|----------|
| Initial loading | Show **Loading authoritative knowledge…** in one neutral bordered panel. Do not show cached Confirmed badges or optimistic hierarchy counts. |
| Mutation pending | Keep the exact reviewed snapshot visible, disable all competing actions, and use the action-specific progress label. Do not replace the panel with a spinner. |
| Idempotent retry | Reuse the client idempotency key for the same logical operation and render the server's authoritative result. Never create a second answer, decision, knowledge version, or activation. |
| Stale revision | Show an inline `role="alert"`: **This item changed in another tab. Your action was not applied. Review the current version before continuing.** Provide **Load current version**. |
| Answer already submitted elsewhere | Render the authoritative Stage 2 snapshot with **Answer submitted in another tab** and remove Stage 1 mutation controls. |
| Decision already recorded elsewhere | Render the immutable recorded decision and references with **This decision was completed in another tab.** Do not offer another confirmation. |
| Active question superseded | Show **This question is no longer active. Your draft was not saved.** Provide **Open active question**. Preserve the superseded question in read-only history. |
| Network outcome unknown | Show **The outcome could not be verified. Nothing will be retried automatically. Check the current version.** Provide **Check current version**, which performs a read only. |
| Partial/malformed response | Treat authority as unknown, hide mutation controls, and show **Authoritative knowledge could not be verified. Reload this view.** |
| Empty proposed list | Heading **No proposed knowledge to review**; body **New research, uploads, imports, edits, and reuse suggestions will appear here before they can become authoritative.** |
| Empty confirmed list | Heading **No confirmed knowledge at this scope**; body **Complete the current interview or promote a reviewed proposal to establish authoritative knowledge.** |
| Empty drift list | Heading **No unresolved knowledge drift**; body **Confirmed knowledge and active configurations currently have no recorded differences requiring owner review.** |
| Read error | Show the exact error-state copy below with **Retry knowledge load**. Never infer authority from client state. |
| Unauthorized | Reuse Phase 1 **Private workspace unavailable** full-page state and reveal no company name, hierarchy, counts, versions, or source references. |

Background refresh never steals focus, discards an unsent draft, or silently swaps the item under review. When a newer revision exists while the owner is editing, freeze the draft, show the stale-revision alert, and require an explicit reload.

## Typography

Use exactly four sizes and two weights: regular `400` and strong `760`.

| Role | Size | Weight | Line Height |
|------|------|--------|-------------|
| Body | 12px | 400 | 1.55 |
| Label | 10px | 760 | 1.2 |
| Heading | 16px | 760 | 1.2 |
| Display / active question | 28px | 760 | 1.05 |

- Labels may use uppercase with `1.15px` letter spacing.
- Digests, version IDs, source references, and audit IDs use Geist Mono at 12px/400 and wrap anywhere.
- Long active questions wrap naturally and remain 28px across desktop and mobile viewports.
- Weight, case, or color alone never communicates Proposed, Confirmed, Active, Superseded, or High-risk status.

## Responsive Contract

- Above 1050px, hierarchy/detail and interview/scope layouts use two columns; the flexible content column always retains at least 640px.
- At 1050px and below, move scope summaries beneath the primary content when the main column would fall below 640px.
- At 760px and below, preserve the existing stacked shell and 16px page inset. Local Knowledge navigation becomes horizontally scrollable with visible text labels; hierarchy uses a native disclosure per level.
- Evidence, inference, recommendation, comparison, and impact sections stack in their semantic order. Never place current and proposed values in an unlabeled side-by-side layout on narrow screens; label **Current confirmed** and **Proposed** above each value.
- At 480px and below, action groups stack to full width. **Accept**, **Reject**, **Correct**, and **Rescope** remain visible without horizontal scrolling.
- No card has a fixed height. Source excerpts, hierarchy names, immutable IDs, and dependency paths wrap without clipping.

## Accessibility Contract

- Use one `h1` for the Knowledge destination, `h2` for the current workflow/card, and `h3` for internal evidence, provenance, and impact sections.
- Local navigation uses links or buttons with `aria-current`; do not implement keyboard-incomplete ARIA tabs.
- The hierarchy is a nested semantic list with buttons; expansion and selection state are announced independently.
- Status badges expose full status words; decorative glyphs use `aria-hidden="true"`.
- Errors and concurrency conflicts use `role="alert"`. Successful load/decision results use one polite live region; do not announce every card or background refresh.
- Enabled controls have a visible 2px focus outline with 2px offset and minimum 44px target height. Focus moves only after an explicit navigation/action or to the first invalid required field after submit.
- When Stage 1 advances to Stage 2, focus the **Confirm submitted answer** heading. When activation succeeds, focus **Replacement active**.
- Native disabled controls include their reason in adjacent visible copy, never only in a tooltip.
- No hover-only provenance, dependency, scope, or conflict information.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; dependency graphs use static connected rows/lines, not animation.
- Source excerpts and untrusted imported text render as text, never raw HTML. External-source links announce that they open in a new tab.
