# Phase 7 greenfield local-only preparation authority

**Status:** Owner-authorized preparation; no Phase 7 plan execution or completion

## Decision

The checked Phase 7 plans remain dependency-gated. Plans 07-01 through 07-03
require completed Plan 06-10, but no `06-10-SUMMARY.md` exists because its
independent-review, Gmail, real-principal, and hosted authorization evidence is
not accepted. Later Phase 7 plans additionally require those plans, persistent
runtime composition, or the non-substitutable archive/clean-target capability
gate in Plan 07-07.

This bounded lane permits pure local synthetic models that make the future
Phase 7 contracts more precise without claiming those predecessors or
executing any Phase 7 plan. No `07-xx-SUMMARY.md` may be created from this lane.

The first preparation slice is a pure synthetic weekly-outcome candidate. It
models the generic first-Export-ready-transition rule in a supplied IANA
timezone and Monday-Sunday local week, retains stable synthetic Prospect and
audit references, and keeps funnel-loss categories separate. It accepts no
contacts, CSV rows, provider data, schedule command, runtime state, or real
identity data.

## Mandatory safeguards

1. Use synthetic IDs, SHA-256 digests, deterministic clocks, and local files
   only. Do not use live/private prospects, contacts, lead files, exports,
   provider observations, or hosted state.
2. Keep every preparation module outside runtime composition. It may not use
   D1/R2, filesystem writes, network/provider ports, routes, workers, UI
   commands, or environment bindings.
3. A modeled transition or digest is not provenance or proof that a real
   outcome occurred. Every artifact must expressly deny operational outcome,
   plan execution, schedule, runner, persistence, export, provider, and effect
   authority.
4. Preserve Phase 4 as the sole schedule authority and Phases 5/6 as the sole
   contact/package/suppression authorities. Do not recreate or assume them.
5. Do not execute Plans 07-01 through 07-10, create a Phase 7 summary, select
   crypto, create an archive, materialize a CSV, provision a target, or earn
   plan/phase credit from preparation evidence.
