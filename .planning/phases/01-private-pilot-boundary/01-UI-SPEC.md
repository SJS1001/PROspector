# Phase 1 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for the private-pilot capability-status and owner-boundary proof surface. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.

---

## Scope and Sources

This phase adds one proof surface to the existing private hosted application. It must let the signed-in owner understand, in plain language, what is **Proven**, **Blocked**, or **Unproven**, why that status applies, what evidence supports it, and what broader operation remains unavailable.

The following are source-of-truth decisions:

- `01-CONTEXT.md`: owner-only workspace, evidence-backed capability states, fail-closed behavior, hosted proof categories, provider-neutral boundaries, and deferred capabilities.
- `REQUIREMENTS.md` and `ROADMAP.md`: human authority, one isolated Company Workspace, auditability, and disabled external effects.
- `site/app/globals.css` and `site/app/prospector-app.tsx`: the established manual brownfield design system, shell, panel, button, banner, responsive, and copy patterns.

Out of scope for this contract:

- Redesigning the Consensus Interview.
- Activating Product discovery, prospecting, enrichment, schedules, runner work, paid work, Gmail, calling, CRM handoff, workspace export, or restore.
- Invitations, user management, or scoped roles.
- Adding shadcn, another component library, or any registry block.

## Information Architecture

### Entry and navigation

1. Add **Pilot Status** as the first primary-navigation destination and make it the default signed-in landing surface for Phase 1.
2. Keep **Knowledge** available because the accepted low-sensitivity Consensus Interview is the only live workflow.
3. Existing later-phase fixture views may remain inspectable only when their synthetic/preview labels remain visible. Every consequential control in those views remains natively disabled.
4. The top-level controlled-pilot banner remains present on every view. On Pilot Status, use:
   - Heading: **Controlled capability pilot**
   - Body: **Only the owner-scoped Consensus Interview is live. Prospecting, contacts, imports, schedules, exports, credentials, Gmail, calling, paid work, and provider effects remain disabled until their own gates are proven.**
5. Never show invitation, add-user, switch-workspace, or multi-user controls.

### Page structure

Render the Pilot Status page in this fixed order:

1. Page heading and overall boundary status.
2. Owner and workspace boundary summary.
3. Capability evidence grid.
4. “What remains unavailable” panel.
5. Audit/evidence explanation.

The owner must be able to understand the overall boundary and all unavailable functions without opening a disclosure.

### Unauthorized and missing identity

When trusted server identity is missing, malformed, conflicting, or unauthorized:

- Render a neutral full-page access state outside the authenticated application shell.
- Do not render the company name, workspace identifiers, capability evidence, navigation, breadcrumbs, counts, or audit references.
- Heading: **Private workspace unavailable**
- Body: **This private pilot could not verify an authorized owner. Sign in with the approved account or contact the pilot administrator.**
- Do not offer workspace selection or an invitation request.

## Status Model

Use exactly these three evidence states. A binding, environment variable, route, or visible control is never sufficient evidence by itself.

| Status | Meaning | Visual treatment | Required content |
|--------|---------|------------------|------------------|
| Proven | Current accepted evidence demonstrates the complete gate | Green text badge on pale-green surface; check glyph plus the word “Proven” | Result, evidence summary, checked time, evidence reference, and what this proof permits |
| Blocked | A known failed check, unavailable prerequisite, or explicit phase boundary prevents proof | Amber text badge on pale-amber surface; alert glyph plus the word “Blocked” | Blocking reason, last known check, evidence reference when available, and what remains unavailable |
| Unproven | No complete accepted proof is recorded, or evidence is incomplete | Muted text badge on paper surface; dash glyph plus the word “Unproven” | Missing evidence, next proof needed, and what remains unavailable |

Status is always communicated by icon/glyph, text, and explanatory copy; never by color alone.

### Overall boundary aggregation

- **Blocked** when any required Phase 1 capability is Blocked.
- **Unproven** when none is Blocked but at least one required Phase 1 capability is Unproven.
- **Proven** only when every required Phase 1 capability is Proven.
- A Proven Phase 1 boundary does not activate later-phase functions. Their own gates remain separate and visibly disabled.
- Loading, request failure, malformed data, or stale/unknown evidence must never render as Proven.

## Component Contract

### Boundary summary

Use one full-width `.panel` immediately below the page heading.

- Left side: overall status badge, one-sentence result, and one-sentence consequence.
- Right side: three compact counts labelled **Proven**, **Blocked**, and **Unproven**.
- The panel border remains `#dfe5df`; do not tint the whole panel green when only some capabilities are Proven.
- The primary action **Review capability evidence** scrolls to and focuses the first Blocked card, otherwise the first Unproven card, otherwise the first Proven card.
- This action is navigational only. It must not run probes, mutate evidence, activate capabilities, or create authority.

### Owner and workspace boundary

Use a two-column panel on desktop and one column on narrow screens.

Left column:

- Label: **OWNER ACCESS**
- Title: **Private pilot owner**
- Result copy states whether trusted server identity is Proven, Blocked, or Unproven.
- Never display raw trusted-header values, email addresses, tokens, or principal identifiers.

Right column:

- Label: **COMPANY WORKSPACE**
- Title: **Digitalrain**
- Copy: **One owner-scoped workspace. Invitations and workspace switching are unavailable.**
- Show route, row, object, and controlled-second-principal proof as separate evidence lines; do not collapse them into one optimistic “isolated” label.

### Capability cards

Render eight cards in this stable order:

1. **Trusted owner identity**
2. **Single-workspace authorization**
3. **D1 durable persistence**
4. **R2 write/read/delete durability**
5. **Route, row, and object isolation**
6. **Mutation protection**
7. **Secrets and audit handling**
8. **Provider-neutral boundary**

Each card contains, in order:

1. Capability name and status badge.
2. A one-sentence plain-language result.
3. **Why** — accepted evidence or the exact missing/failed prerequisite.
4. **Unavailable while not proven** — the dependent capability or data class that stays disabled.
5. A native disclosure labelled **View evidence**.

The expanded evidence disclosure contains only:

- **Demonstrated:** the specific accepted check and outcome.
- **Checked:** localized date and time in `America/Toronto`, plus an unambiguous ISO timestamp in accessible text.
- **Environment:** a safe environment/deployment label.
- **Reference:** opaque audit/evidence identifier in Geist Mono.
- **Boundary effect:** what becomes safe to rely on, not a claim that later workflow gates passed.

Do not expose secrets, credentials, raw identity headers, object contents, operational lead/contact data, or full principal identifiers in evidence details.

### What remains unavailable

Use one full-width panel titled **Broader operation remains disabled**. Render these rows with a visible **Blocked** badge and reason:

| Capability group | Required reason |
|------------------|-----------------|
| Product discovery and prospecting | Product and Profile readiness are not proven; only synthetic fixtures may be shown |
| Leads, contacts, and imports | No authenticated live-data intake or eligibility gate is active |
| Schedules, runners, and paid work | Scheduler, assignment, callback, quota, and spend-authority gates are not proven |
| Credentials and provider effects | Controlled provider-account and secrets-use proof is not complete |
| Gmail, calling, and outreach | Package, message, freshness, suppression, compliance, and final-effect gates are not proven |
| CRM handoff and workspace export/restore | Live eligibility, encryption, delivery, integrity, and clean-restore proof belong to Phase 7 |

If a disabled control is rendered beside a row, use the native `disabled` attribute and repeat the reason in visible text. A `title` tooltip alone is insufficient.

### Audit explanation

End the page with a compact neutral note:

**What counts as proof?** A capability is Proven only when its complete accepted hosted check is recorded. Configuration presence and UI availability do not count. Failed, partial, missing, or unreadable evidence keeps the capability disabled.

## Interaction and State Contract

| State | Contract |
|-------|----------|
| Loading | Show **Checking accepted capability evidence…** in a neutral bordered panel. Do not show cached counts or green placeholders. |
| Loaded | Announce the overall status once through a polite live region; do not announce every card independently. |
| Evidence disclosure | Use native `<details>`/`<summary>` semantics. Opening is local UI state and does not trigger a capability probe. |
| Refresh | A refresh may repeat the non-cacheable read only. It must not perform storage writes, external calls, or proof mutations. |
| Error | Show the exact error-state copy below with a **Retry status check** button. All broader operation remains disabled. |
| Unauthorized | Use the neutral access state defined above and reveal no workspace data. |
| Partial/malformed response | Treat affected cards as Unproven and show **Accepted evidence was incomplete.** Never infer Proven from another card. |
| Disabled later workflow | Keep controls natively disabled and show the blocking reason adjacent to the control. |

Focus must move only for the explicit **Review capability evidence** action. Background refresh must not reorder cards or steal focus.

## Spacing Scale

New Phase 1 proof-surface elements use this declared scale:

| Token | Value | Usage |
|-------|-------|-------|
| xs | 4px | Glyph-to-label gap and compact metadata separation |
| sm | 8px | Badge padding rhythm and evidence-row gaps |
| md | 16px | Default card gap and mobile page inset |
| lg | 24px | Card padding and section separation |
| xl | 32px | Desktop section separation |
| 2xl | 48px | Major page grouping |
| 3xl | 64px | Reserved page-level spacing |

Exceptions inherited from the established shell:

- Fixed rail width: `248px`.
- Sticky top bar height: `68px`.
- Content horizontal inset: `4.2vw` desktop.
- Panel radius: `9px`; button radius: `7px`; status pill radius: `12px`.
- Enabled action targets must be at least `44px` high even where the existing visual button padding is more compact.

Do not introduce new arbitrary spacing values in the capability surface.

## Typography

The Phase 1 proof surface declares four roles and exactly two weights: regular `400` and strong `760`.

| Role | Size | Weight | Line Height |
|------|------|--------|-------------|
| Body | 12px | 400 | 1.55 |
| Label | 10px | 760 | 1.2 |
| Heading | 16px | 760 | 1.2 |
| Display | 28px | 760 | 1.05 |

- Labels may use uppercase with `1.15px` letter spacing.
- Evidence references use Geist Mono at the Body size and must wrap anywhere.
- Do not use font weight alone to communicate status.
- Existing screens outside this proof surface retain their current type rules.

## Copywriting Contract

| Element | Copy |
|---------|------|
| Page eyebrow | OWNER-ONLY · CONTROLLED PILOT |
| Page title | Private pilot boundary |
| Page introduction | See what is proven, what is blocked, and what remains unproven before any broader operation can begin. |
| Primary CTA | Review capability evidence |
| Overall Proven | All required Phase 1 controls have accepted evidence. Later workflow gates remain separate and disabled. |
| Overall Blocked | The private pilot boundary is blocked. Review the failed or unavailable proof below; broader operation remains disabled. |
| Overall Unproven | The private pilot boundary is not proven yet. Missing evidence is listed below; broader operation remains disabled. |
| Empty state heading | No capability evidence is available |
| Empty state body | Proof has not been recorded. Every capability remains unproven and broader operation stays disabled. |
| Error state | Capability status could not be loaded. Nothing has been enabled. Retry the status check. |
| Error CTA | Retry status check |
| Unauthorized heading | Private workspace unavailable |
| Unauthorized body | This private pilot could not verify an authorized owner. Sign in with the approved account or contact the pilot administrator. |
| Destructive confirmation | None — this proof surface is read-only and exposes no destructive action |

### Card sentence patterns

- Proven: **{Capability} is proven. {Specific accepted result}.**
- Blocked: **{Capability} is blocked because {specific reason}. {Dependent function} remains unavailable.**
- Unproven: **{Capability} is unproven because accepted evidence has not been recorded. {Specific proof} is still required.**

Use “Proven,” “Blocked,” and “Unproven” exactly. Do not substitute vague labels such as “Ready,” “Connected,” “Configured,” “Healthy,” or “Available.”

## Responsive Contract

- At widths above `1050px`, render the capability grid in two equal columns.
- At `1050px` and below, retain two columns only while every card has at least `320px` available; otherwise use one column.
- At `760px` and below, preserve the existing stacked shell, `16px` page inset, horizontal navigation behavior, and single-column capability/owner-boundary layout.
- At `480px` and below, stack overall-status counts and actions. Status labels and reasons must remain visible without horizontal scrolling.
- Do not give cards fixed heights. Evidence text and identifiers must wrap without clipping.
- The stable card order must not change across breakpoints.

---

## Accessibility Contract

- Use one `h1` for **Private pilot boundary**, `h2` for major sections, and `h3` for capability cards.
- Each badge exposes the full status word to assistive technology; decorative glyphs are `aria-hidden`.
- Use `role="status"`/`aria-live="polite"` only for the overall load result and `role="alert"` for errors.
- All enabled controls have a visible `2px` focus outline with `2px` offset and a minimum `44px` target height.
- Disabled controls use native `disabled`; their reason is visible in the document, not available only on hover.
- No hover-only evidence, status, or explanation.
- Respect the existing `prefers-reduced-motion` rule. No pulsing or animated status indicators are allowed on this proof surface.
- Maintain text and control contrast against the specified surfaces. Semantic color never carries meaning without text.
