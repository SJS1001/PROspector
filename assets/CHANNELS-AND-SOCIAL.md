## 1. Which channels do you actually need?

Prioritized for B2B critical-minerals/heavy-industry — not consumer reach.

| Channel | Need? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Company LinkedIn page** | **Essential** | Your buyers (COO, Plant/Met Manager, Operating Partner) live here. Prospects check it before replying to outbound. Hosts your content engine. |
| **Founder/key-person LinkedIn** | **Essential** | In B2B, personal profiles outperform company pages on reach. Your Rolls-Royce/aerospace pedigree lands hardest as a *person's* story. Most replies will come via founder posts + DMs. |
| **Website** | Have it — **tighten it** | See §5. Needs a demo-request CTA, proof, and a security page. |
| **YouTube (unlisted/public)** | **Recommended** | Host the 2-min product walkthrough + a masked Engebø demo. Link from email/LinkedIn. Low effort, high trust. |
| **X/Twitter** | Optional | Some mining-tech/metallurgy community exists, but thin ROI vs. LinkedIn. Only if you'll actually post. |
| **Instagram / TikTok / Facebook** | **Skip** | Wrong audience for an OT decision layer. Don't spread thin. |
| **Industry presence (AusIMM, SME, mining media)** | **Recommended later** | Guest articles / conference talks build more credibility than social with this audience. |

**Rule:** run **two** profiles well (company + founder) before adding a third channel. Consistency beats coverage.

## 5. What else you need — GTM asset gap checklist

You have: brochure, deck, website. Prioritized gaps:

**P0 — before serious outbound**
- **Company + founder LinkedIn** (above) — live and complete.
- **One-pager / leave-behind** (PDF) — problem → ONE → 3 differentiators → Engebø proof → CTA. (Outlined in SALES-MARKETING-PLAN.md §6.)
- **Security one-pager** — read-only, segmented OT/IT, client-owned data + models; for the OT/Controls validator and CISO. This unblocks deals; build it early.
- **Website essentials:** a clear demo-request/contact CTA, a proof/credibility line, and a short security/trust section. Make `hello@digitalrain.ai` and a "book a diagnostic" path obvious.
- **Email signature + domain email** on digitalrain.ai (not a generic inbox) — deliverability + credibility.

**P1 — to convert interest**
- **2-min product walkthrough video** (screen capture of ONE) + a **masked Engebø demo** clip. Host on YouTube; embed on site, pin on LinkedIn.
- **Nordic Mining reference-call kit** — NDA + a short question set (your most verifiable proof; see ICP/battlecard).
- **ROI / value worksheet** — inputs (nameplate %, recovery %, downtime hrs, throughput value) → value at stake; frames the diagnostic without over-claiming.
- **Persona one-pagers / cards** (champion, economic buyer, greenfield, OT validator) — from SALES-MARKETING-PLAN.md §2.

**P2 — to scale credibility**
- **Case study** (Engebø, within NDA — normalized/relative metrics) or a "how the diagnostic works" methodology piece as a stand-in until a second reference lands.
- **CRM** (even lightweight) — the real missing system to hold long mining sales cycles; pairs with the nightly agent + Apollo.
- **Lead magnet** — e.g. "The ramp-up loss calculator" or "What a decision layer actually is" guide, to capture inbound from the social content.
- **Guest article / conference talk** (AusIMM, SME, mining media) — outperforms social for this audience.
- **Second reference customer** — the single highest-leverage credibility asset (flagged in the red-team).
