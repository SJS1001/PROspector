# ONE — the decision layer for mine uptime, recovery and throughput

**The problem.** The data to run a processing plant well already exists — it's just trapped in OEM systems, historians and vendor portals. Failures surface late and decisions run hours behind the process. The constraint isn't sensing. It's access and decision latency.

**What ONE does.** ONE sits on top of the systems you already run and closes the loop: it ingests your equipment, process, lab and maintenance data, harmonizes it into one operating model, and puts a clear recommendation — with the evidence — in front of the right person in time to act. Historians store and dashboards show; **ONE decides and routes the work.**

**Why ONE is different**
- **A decision layer, not another dashboard** — turns trapped data into an action, on top of your existing stack.
- **Vendor-neutral** — unifies OEM, historian and lab silos into one view you own.
- **Read-only & yours** — connects read-only over a segmented OT/IT link; your data and the models trained on it stay with you.
- **Reliability pedigree** — built by a team from aerospace and maritime, where an unplanned failure grounds a fleet, not just a shift.

**Proof.** ONE is in live production at Engebø with Nordic Mining, running on real plant data. (Engebø figures kept confidential for client data security; reference call available under NDA.)

**Where to start.** A fixed-scope, **eight-week diagnostic on one decision**, run on your own data:
weeks 1–2 connect two or three priority systems (read-only) · weeks 3–5 model one high-value decision · weeks 6–8 measure your site-specific baseline and the value at stake.
